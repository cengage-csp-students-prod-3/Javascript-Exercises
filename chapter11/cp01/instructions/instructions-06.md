Open _bw_review.html_ in your browser. Verify that as you move your mouse pointer over the stars below the book description, the rating value automatically matches your selection. Also verify that moving the mouse pointer off the star images removes the rating unless you have clicked one of the stars. Finally, verify that as you type characters into the text area box, a running count of the character total is shown below the box and, if the number of characters exceeds 1000, the total is displayed in a white font on a red background.
